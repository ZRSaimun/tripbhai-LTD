## Services and regulatory scope

The register’s SIC code is a classification, not evidence of a specific service, trading volume, adviser authorisation or outcome record. The product brief prioritises visit-visa enquiries, flights, hotels, tailored travel planning and practical one-to-one UK orientation. It prohibits invented eligibility requirements, fees, processing times, live fares, customer records, success rates, awards and guarantees.

GOV.UK says immigration advisers must be registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) or belong to an approved professional body. The guidance explicitly includes help filling in forms among adviser activities. Keep UK advice, form selection and representation unavailable until the responsible professional’s registration and permitted scope are recorded and verified. The application has a server-side authorisation gate; public copy must not imply the company is authorised when no evidence has been verified.
