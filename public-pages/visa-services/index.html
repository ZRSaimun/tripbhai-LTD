A few helpful answers.

Does an enquiry submit my visa application?

No. It starts a conversation with Trip Bhai. An application to an embassy or authority is a separate step.

Can Trip Bhai guarantee an approval?

No. Decisions are made by the relevant authorities. A previous approval is not a guarantee for another application.

What does the service cost?

We confirm the scope and quotation for your enquiry before work begins. No fee or processing time is promised on this page.

Where can I see visa stories?

Visit our visa stories page. Individual outcomes should never be treated as a prediction for your own case.
