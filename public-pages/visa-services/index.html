HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Do I need to send my passport with the first enquiry?

No. Start with basic contact details and an outline of your plans. If documents are needed, the team will provide a relevant checklist through the secure client portal.

Does an enquiry mean I have applied for a visa?

No. It is an enquiry to Trip Bhai. Any application to a government authority is a separate step.

Can Trip Bhai guarantee a visa?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities. Past outcomes cannot guarantee a future decision.

Will I see live appointment availability or flight prices here?

No. This page does not show live fares, appointment slots or availability. These need to be confirmed separately.
