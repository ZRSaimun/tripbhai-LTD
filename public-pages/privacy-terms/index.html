How this website works with your information, and what you can expect when you make an enquiry with TRIP BHAI LTD.

Privacy on
this website.

This public website is a set of static pages. It does not set tracking cookies, run analytics scripts or save your enquiry or chat messages. The automated chat processes questions on your device, and closing or reloading the page clears the conversation.

The enquiry form on our contact page does not send anything by itself. It prepares a message on your device which you review and send from your own email app to tripbhaiofficial@gmail.com. Until you send that email, we receive nothing.

When you email or call us, we use your contact details and the travel details you share to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to provide the agreed service. We ask you not to send passport scans, bank statements or other sensitive documents in a first enquiry. Sensitive documents are only requested through the separate client portal, which has its own privacy information.

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep. Email tripbhaiofficial@gmail.com.

Service terms
in brief.

01

An enquiry is not a booking

Sending us an enquiry does not reserve a flight, hotel or holiday and is not an application to any embassy, consulate or government authority. We confirm availability, prices, supplier terms and next steps separately, in writing.

02

Scope and cost agreed first

Before any paid work begins we confirm what the service includes, what it does not include and the fee. No fee, processing time or timescale is promised on this website.

03

No guaranteed outcomes

Visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities. We cannot guarantee an approval, and a past approval shown on our visa stories page is not a prediction for anyone else’s application.

04

UK immigration advice

UK immigration advice, choosing an application form and representation are not offered through this website. See our service availability page. Practical orientation support, where offered, is not legal advice, therapy or crisis support.

05

Photographs and stories

Client photographs are published only with the client’s permission. To withdraw a photograph or story, email us and we will remove it from the public site.

06

Changes

We may update this page as our services change. The version published on this website is the current one. Last reviewed: November 2026.
